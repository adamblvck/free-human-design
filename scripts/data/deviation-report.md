## Notes & caveats

- **Pluto drives the era-dependent divergence** (see the by-body table): its long-term
  orbit is the hardest to approximate, so free-human-design's precession-based Pluto drifts
  from Swiss Ephemeris by tens of arcminutes at the window extremes. The lunar nodes are
  a distant second. The Sun, Moon and the other planets stay well inside a gate line.
- Both engines convert the *same local time + IANA timezone* to UTC independently. Before
  ~1900 the IANA database uses Local Mean Time and ΔT is less certain; part of the early
  and far-future p95/max Δ is timezone/ΔT policy, not ephemeris. Median (robust to those
  outliers) is the headline stat for that reason.
- Longitudes are geocentric tropical; the study assumes the official engine is geocentric too.
